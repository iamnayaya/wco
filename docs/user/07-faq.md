# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Quick answers to the questions merchants ask most. For step-by-step help, use the guides links.

## Account & login

**How do I create an account?**
Sign up at **app.wco.com** or in the mobile app with email or phone. Confirm your email/phone. → [Getting started](./01-getting-started.md)

**I forgot my password.**
On the login screen tap **Forgot password** → check your email/SMS → set a new one. → [Troubleshooting](./troubleshooting.md)

**Can I use WCO on my phone?**
Yes — install the [mobile app](./mobile-app-guide.md).

**Is my data safe?**
Yes. WCO uses strong encryption, secure sign-in (with optional 2FA), and follows [privacy laws](./compliance.md). See [Security](./security.md).

## Store & products

**How fast can I set up my store?**
About **5 minutes** for a basic catalog, WhatsApp, and AI. → [Quick start](./02-quick-start.md)

**How many products can I add?**
Practically unlimited, based on your plan. Bulk import with CSV works well for large catalogs. → [Products guide](./guides/products-guide.md)

**How do I change prices?**
Products → open product → edit price → Save. The AI then answers with the new price automatically.

**Can I offer sizes/colors?**
Yes — use **variants** on a product. → [Products guide](./guides/products-guide.md)

## WhatsApp & AI

**How does the AI answer my customers?**
The AI reads your product catalog and response templates to answer common questions in ~5 seconds, and hands off to you for sales. → [Messages / AI guide](./guides/messages-guide.md)

**What if the AI gives a wrong answer?**
Take over the conversation, correct the product info, and improve your templates. The AI uses your current catalog data.

**Does the AI reply while I'm asleep?**
Yes — if enabled, it auto-replies 24/7, which is great for capturing sales while you sleep.

**Do I need to keep WhatsApp linked?**
Yes — the WhatsApp connection is what lets customers message you through WCO.

## Orders, payments & delivery

**How do customers pay me?**
You send a **payment link**; customers pay via Paystack, Flutterwave, or OPay (card, USSD, bank, mobile money). → [Payments guide](./guides/payments-guide.md)

**When do I get my money?**
Provider payouts usually arrive next day (T+1) into your connected bank account.

**How does delivery work?**
Connect GIG/Kwik/Sendy in Settings → Delivery, request a quote, and book. → [Deliveries guide](./guides/deliveries-guide.md)

**Can a customer pay without WhatsApp?**
They can pay via a payment link you send by any channel.

## Billing & plans

**How much does WCO cost?**
See the **Pricing/Plans** page. We have free and paid tiers based on features and order volume.

**How do I upgrade my plan?**
Settings → **Billing/Subscription**.

**Can I cancel anytime?**
Yes — cancel in Settings → Billing. Your data remains exportable.

## Privacy & data

**Can customers request their data?**
Yes — per GDPR/NDPR, customers can request access, correction, or deletion. → [Compliance](./compliance.md)

**Can I export my store data?**
Yes — use **Settings → Privacy → Export**.

## Technical

**What happens if WCO is down?**
Check https://status.wco.com for status and updates.

**Is there an API?**
Yes — a full public API is available for partners/integrators. → [API docs](../api/README.md)
