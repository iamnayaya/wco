# Tips & Tricks

Battle-tested advice from successful merchants and our team. These patterns measurably increase sales and save time.

## Grow sales

1. **Turn on the AI 24/7.** Capturing inquiries while you sleep = sales you'd otherwise miss. Most merchants see a jump in after-hours conversions.
2. **Keep prices & stock fresh.** The AI can only be accurate if your catalog is. Update stock as you sell.
3. **Add photos to every product.** WhatsApp messages with product photos convert far better. One good photo beats a long description.
4. **Answer unanswered messages first.** Every unanswered customer is a potential lost sale. Build a habit: check the **Unanswered** filter each morning.
5. **Create bundles / combos.** "Rice + beans + oil" sets raise average order value (AOV).
6. **Offer free delivery over a threshold.** It nudges customers to add more to their cart.

## Save time

7. **Use CSV import** to load a big catalog fast — don't type each product. → [Products guide](./guides/products-guide.md)
8. **Use templates** for the AI and for your own replies so you don't retype common answers.
9. **Reorder past orders** in one tap for stocking repeat customers. → [Orders guide](./guides/orders-guide.md)
10. **Segment customers** ("VIP", "Wholesale") for targeted messages. → [Customers guide](./guides/customers-guide.md)
11. **Turn on notifications** so you never miss an order or payment, even away from your laptop.

## Use the data

12. **Watch product analytics** to know what to restock first (fast-movers) and what to discount (slow-movers).
13. **Use the date-range filter** to compare this week to last week — catch trends early.
14. **Check customer repeat rate** — a healthy store grows repeat buyers. Send a thank-you or small loyalty perk to top customers.

## WhatsApp & AI best practices

15. **Customize AI templates** in your own voice and language so replies feel like *you*. → [Messages guide](./guides/messages-guide.md)
16. **Set handoff rules** so buying-intent messages always reach you — never risk the AI fumbling a serious order.
17. **Program the AI for FAQs** (delivery time, payment options, opening hours) so it handles the boring stuff.
18. **Take over conversations** when personal touch matters (big orders, complaints, regulars).

## Payments & delivery shortcuts

19. **Offer multiple payment providers** — customers pay with whatever they have.
20. **Pre-set delivery rates** so checkout is instant and customers aren't surprised.
21. **Share the tracking link** automatically — customers love knowing where their order is.

## Staying safe

22. **Turn on two-factor authentication (2FA).** → [Settings guide](./guides/settings-guide.md)
23. **Invite staff with their own logins** instead of sharing your password — you keep control and an audit trail. Assign the least privilege needed (Agent vs Admin).
24. **Only broadcast to opted-in customers**, and include an opt-out word (reply "STOP"). → [Customers guide](./guides/customers-guide.md#marketing-responsibly)

## Keyboard / interface shortcuts (web)

- **Search** product/order/customer quickly with the global search box.
- Use **filters** to jump straight to Unanswered / Paid / In-transit.
- Bookmark frequently used pages.
