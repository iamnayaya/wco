# User Guide: Privacy & Compliance

A plain-language overview of how WCO handles data privacy and our compliance commitments. The full [Compliance documentation](../compliance/README.md) is for compliance, legal, and DPO teams.

## Our privacy commitments

WCO is built **privacy-first** and complies with the data-protection laws of the markets we serve, including:

- **GDPR** (EU General Data Protection Regulation)
- **NDPR** (Nigeria Data Protection Regulation)
- **POPIA** (South Africa Protection of Personal Information Act)
- And equivalent local laws in Ghana, Kenya, and beyond

## Key principles

1. **Transparency** — we tell you what data we collect and why.
2. **Purpose limitation** — we use data only for what you've agreed to (running your store).
3. **Data minimization** — we collect only what we need.
4. **Security** — strong technical controls protect data.
5. **User rights** — you (and your customers) have rights over data.

## Your store's data

- **What we collect:** store info, products, orders, customers, conversations, payment/delivery records — all needed to run your store.
- **Who can see it:** you and the staff members you invite (based on their roles).
- **Export:** you can download a copy anytime via **Settings → Privacy → Export**.
- **Retention:** data is kept only as long as needed for business and legal/tax reasons. See [Data retention](../compliance/04-data-retention.md).

## Your customers' data & rights

Your customers have rights over their personal data that you (as the "controller") should honor:

| Right | Meaning | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Access** | See what data you hold | Customer requests; you can share their profile |
| **Correction** | Fix inaccurate data | Update their profile |
| **Deletion** | Erase their data | Delete their profile (order records may be kept for tax/legal) |
| **Object / Opt-out** | Stop marketing | Reply "STOP" or remove from broadcasts |

WCO provides tools to fulfill these requests (profiles, delete, export). Contact **support@wco.com** if you need help honoring a customer's request.

## Marketing responsibly

- Only **broadcast** to customers who've opted in.
- Include a way to **opt out** (reply "STOP").
- Respect quiet hours and local regulations.

## Where your data is stored (data residency)

Your data is stored in secure cloud data centers (e.g., AWS regions), with safeguards appropriate to the region. See [Data residency](../compliance/03-data-residency.md).
