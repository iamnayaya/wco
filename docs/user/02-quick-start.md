# Quick Start — Your First Sale in 10 Minutes

This is the fastest path from "just signed up" to "I made a sale." Each step points to a detailed guide.

## The 10-minute plan

| Minute | Step |
|---|---|
| 0–1 | Sign up & confirm your account |
| 1–3 | Set up your store (name, category, currency, WhatsApp) |
| 3–5 | Add 2–3 products |
| 5–6 | Connect WhatsApp number |
| 6–8 | Turn on the AI auto-responder |
| 8–10 | Test — message your own WhatsApp number and complete a test order |

## Step-by-step

### 1. Sign up
Go to **app.wco.com → Sign up**, confirm your email or phone. → [Getting started](./01-getting-started.md#1-create-your-account)

### 2. Set up your store
**Settings → Store** (or the setup wizard after sign-up). Add your store name, category, currency, and delivery area. → [Getting started](./01-getting-started.md#2-set-up-your-store)

### 3. Add products
**Products → + Add product.** Name, price, stock, photo. → [Products guide](./guides/products-guide.md)

```mermaid

    A[Add Product] --> B[Set Price]
    B --> C[Add Stock]

```

### 4. Connect WhatsApp
**Settings → WhatsApp → Connect number → scan QR.** → [Getting started](./01-getting-started.md#4-connect-whatsapp)

### 5. Turn on the AI auto-responder
**Settings → AI (or Messages).** Enable auto-reply, choose a default response template, and let the AI answer product questions from your catalog. → [Messages / AI guide](./guides/messages-guide.md)

> The AI reads your product catalog to answer accurately and can hand off to you when it can't help or the customer wants to buy.

### 6. Test end-to-end
Message your connected WhatsApp number from another phone:

1. Ask *"how much is your rice?"* → AI replies with price/stock.
2. Say *"I want to buy"* → payment link is generated.
3. Open the link and pay (use a test/sandbox if available).
4. Open the **Orders** page → see the new order.
5. Arrange **delivery** in Orders (or mark as pickup). → [Orders guide](./guides/orders-guide.md), [Payments guide](./guides/payments-guide.md)

**You just completed your first WCO sale! 🎉**

## Checklist after your first sale

- [ ] Add your full product catalog (photo + stock per item).
- [ ] Review your AI response templates for accuracy & brand voice.
- [ ] Add a payout account in **Settings → Payments** so you can withdraw.
- [ ] Set your delivery rates in **Settings → Delivery**.
- [ ] Explore the **Analytics** page to see your first sales trend.

## Common quick fixes

| Issue | Fix |
|---|---|
| AI not replying | Check WhatsApp is connected + AI enabled in Settings → AI |
| Can't receive payments | Add payout account; confirm PSP credentials in Settings → Payments |
| Order shows as "draft" | Mark it confirmed; then arrange delivery/payment |
| Wrong currency | Change in Settings → Store |

For anything else → [Troubleshooting](../troubleshooting/README.md) or support@wco.com.
