# Getting Started — User Guide

Welcome to **WhatsApp Commerce OS (WCO)**! This guide gets you from zero to your first sale. WCO helps you run your WhatsApp-based business: accept orders, take payments, arrange delivery, manage customers, and let AI handle routine customer questions.

## What you can do with WCO

- 🌟 Set up your store in about **5 minutes**.
- 📱 Run everything from a web dashboard or the mobile app.
- 🤖 Let the **AI auto-responder** answer customer questions in ~5 seconds.
- 💳 Receive payments via **Paystack, Flutterwave, or OPay** in about a minute.
- 🚚 Arrange delivery with **GIG, Kwik, or Sendy** in about two minutes.
- 👥 Track customers and conversations 24/7.
- 📊 See real-time sales and customer analytics.

## 1. Create your account

1. Go to **https://app.wco.com** (or open the mobile app).
2. Tap **Sign up**.
3. Choose how to register — email or phone number.
4. Create a password, or use your preferred sign-in.
5. Confirm your email/phone (check your inbox/SMS) and you're in.

> Need help? See [Account & login troubleshooting](../troubleshooting/01-common-issues.md#login-issues).

## 2. Set up your store

After sign-up you'll be walked through a short setup (you can also do this later in **Settings → Store**):

1. **Store name** — e.g., "Grace's Groceries".
2. **Business category** — e.g., groceries, fashion, electronics.
3. **Currency** — e.g., Naira (NGN), Cedi (GHS), Shilling (KES).
4. **Location / delivery area** — where you deliver.
5. **Connect WhatsApp** — link the WhatsApp number you do business on (see [Setup WhatsApp](#4-connect-whatsapp)).

## 3. Add your first products

1. Open the **Products** page in the left menu.
2. Tap **+ Add product**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Name** (e.g., "5kg Rice")
   - **Price** (in your store currency)
   - **Stock** (how many you have)
   - **Photo** (optional but recommended)
   - **Description** (optional)
4. Tap **Save**.

Repeat for everything you sell. See the [Products guide](./guides/products-guide.md) for variants, bundles, and bulk upload.

> **Tip:** Products with photos convert far better in WhatsApp chats. Add one great photo per product.

## 4. Connect WhatsApp

This is what makes WCO special — customers can order straight from WhatsApp.

1. Go to **Settings → WhatsApp**.
2. Tap **Connect number**, enter the number you want to use.
3. Scan the QR code with WhatsApp to link the number.
4. Confirm the connection.

Now customers who message that WhatsApp number will reach you through WCO, and the AI can auto-respond to routine questions.

> Meta's approval for business messaging applies; WCO walks you through the (small) setup. See [Deliveries & Messaging](../troubleshooting/01-common-issues.md#whatsapp-issues).

## 5. Receive your first order

A customer messages your WhatsApp: *"How much is your 5kg rice?"*

- The **AI auto-responder** answers with the price and availability instantly (or routes to you).
- When the customer says they want to order, a **payment link** can be generated automatically or by you.
- The customer pays via Paystack/Flutterwave/OPay.
- You see the **order** in the Orders page, arrange **delivery**, and it's done!

See [Orders guide](./guides/orders-guide.md) and [Payments guide](./guides/payments-guide.md).
