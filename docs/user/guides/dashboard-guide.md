# Dashboard Guide

The dashboard is your home base — a real-time overview of how your store is doing, with quick links to everything.

## What's on the dashboard

![Dashboard overview — screenshot description: shows KPI cards (revenue, orders, customers), a sales chart, recent orders, and a WhatsApp activity panel]

### KPI cards
- **Revenue** — today's and this week's revenue (in your store currency).
- **Orders** — total orders, plus new/processing counts.
- **Customers** — total customers, new customers this week.
- **Messages** — incoming WhatsApp messages, unanswered count (needs your attention).

### Charts
- **Sales trend** — revenue over time (today / 7 days / 30 days).
- Optionally: **Top products** and **customer growth**.

### Lists
- **Recent orders** — latest orders with status; tap to open.
- **WhatsApp activity** — latest conversations (especially any the AI didn't handle).

## Reading the dashboard

| Widget | What it tells you | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Unanswered messages badge | Customers waiting on you | Open Messages and reply |
| Low-stock product alerts | Items about to run out | Open Products and restock |
| Pending orders | Orders needing confirmation/delivery | Open Orders |
| Revenue spike/dip | What's selling (or not) | Open Analytics for detail |

## Using the navigation

- **Dashboard** — this page.
- **Products** — manage your catalog.
- **Orders** — manage orders & fulfillment.
- **Customers** — see and manage your customer base.
- **Messages** — WhatsApp conversations + AI auto-responder.
- **Analytics** — deeper sales/customer/product reports.
- **Settings** — store, WhatsApp, payments, delivery, AI, notifications.

## Pro tips

- Check the **unanswered messages** badge first thing — every unanswered customer is a potential lost sale.
- Use the **date-range filter** to compare week-over-week performance.
- Pin your most-viewed page by bookmarking the URL.
