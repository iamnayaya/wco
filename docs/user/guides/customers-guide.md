# Customers Guide

Understand and manage the people who buy from you. WCO's customer management (CRM) helps you build relationships, personalize service, and grow repeat business.

## What a customer profile contains

- **Contact** — name, phone/WhatsApp, email (if provided).
- **Conversations** — their message history.
- **Orders** — their order history and lifetime value.
- **Segments & tags** — groupings you define (e.g., "VIP", "New", "Lagos").
- **Notes** — your private notes about the customer.

## Viewing customers

- **Customers** page lists everyone who has contacted/ordered.
- **Search** by name or phone number.
- **Sort / filter** by recency, order count, and value.

## Adding & updating customers

- Customers are usually **auto-created** when they message you or place an order.
- **Add manually** with **+ New customer**.
- Tap a customer to edit their details, add notes, or assign tags.

## Segments & tags

### Tags
Quick labels on a customer — e.g., `returns`, `wholesale`, `prefers-pickup`. Apply one or many.

### Segments
Saved, dynamic groupings based on rules — e.g., "Customers who bought in the last 30 days" or "Orders over ₦50,000". Segments update automatically and are the basis for **targeted campaigns / broadcast messages**.

## Using customer data

- **Personalized replies** — greet customers by name, recall their past orders ("same rice as last time?").
- **Marketing** — send thank-you or promotion messages to a segment.
- **Analytics** — customer lifetime value, repeat rate, top customers.

## Types of customers (informal-market friendly)

- **Cash/walk-in customers** — you can create a lightweight profile from a phone number.
- **WhatsApp customers** — full profile with chat history.
- **Wholesale/bulk buyers** — mark with a tag/segment to prioritize.

## Privacy & consent (important)

- WCO is built **privacy-first** and complies with **GDPR/NDPR** ([Compliance docs](../compliance/README.md)).
- Only use customer data to serve the customer and your business.
- Customers may request to see or delete their data — see **Settings → Privacy** or contact support.
- Do **not** share customer data or use it for unrelated purposes.

## Marketing responsibly

- Only send **broadcast** messages to customers who've opted in.
- Provide a way to opt out (e.g., reply "STOP").
- Respect local timing (don't message late at night).
