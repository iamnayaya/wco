# Products Guide

Manage everything you sell. A good catalog = more sales and a smarter AI auto-responder.

## Adding a product

1. Open **Products → + Add product**.
2. Fill in the details:

| Field | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Name | ✅ | e.g., "5kg Parboiled Rice" |
| Price | ✅ | Major units, in your store currency |
| Stock | ✅ | Quantity available |
| Photo | Recommended | Drives conversions; one strong photo |
| Description | Optional | Useful to the AI for answering questions |
| Category | Optional | Helps customers and analytics |

3. Tap **Save**.

## Editing a product

- Tap a product to open it, edit any field, and **Save**.
- Adjust stock quickly with **+ / −** buttons (selling fast? increment).
- Set a product **inactive** to hide it without deleting.

## Variants & options

For products that come in sizes/colors/flavors:

- Tap **Add variant** on the product.
- Example: T-shirt with **Size** (S/M/L) and **Color** (Black/White).
- Each variant can have its own price and stock.

## Categories & organization

- Create categories (e.g., Grains, Beverages, Fashion) under **Products → Categories**.
- Assign products to categories.
- Categories also power **analytics** (top category revenue) and help the AI answer "what do you have?"

## Bulk upload

Have a spreadsheet? Use **Products → Import**:

1. Download the **CSV template**.
2. Fill in product name, price, stock, category, description.
3. Upload the CSV. Review the preview, fix any errors, **Import**.

> A CSV example row: `name,price,stock,category,description` → `5kg Rice,15000,50,Grains,Long-grain parboiled`

## Searching & filtering

- **Search** by product name in the search box.
- **Filter** by category, stock status (in stock / low / out), or active/inactive.
- Sort by name, price, or newest.

## Low-stock alerts

- Set a **low-stock threshold** per product (or a default in Settings).
- When stock drops below it, you'll see an alert on the dashboard and (optionally) a notification.

## Deleting products

- **Delete** only removes the product from the catalog.
- Past orders keep their records (product history is preserved in order items) — you won't lose order data.
- Prefer **inactive** over delete when you want to keep history but stop selling.

## How products power the AI

The AI auto-responder reads your product catalog to answer customer questions:

- *"Do you have rice?"* → answers with your rice products, prices, and stock.
- *"How much is delivery?"* → uses your delivery rates.

**Keep prices and stock up to date** so the AI (and you) always give accurate answers. → [Messages / AI guide](./messages-guide.md).

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Fix |
|---|---|
| Product not showing in WhatsApp | Ensure it's **active** with stock > 0 |
| Wrong price shown by AI | Update the product price; the AI uses current data |
| Import failed | Check the CSV for missing required columns (name/price/stock) |

Need more? → [Troubleshooting](../troubleshooting/README.md)
