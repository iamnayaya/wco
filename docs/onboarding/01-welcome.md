# Onboarding — Welcome

Welcome to **WhatsApp Commerce OS (WCO)**! We're building an AI-powered commerce OS for informal traders in emerging markets — and you're now part of the team that makes it real.

## Who we are
- **Mission:** help informal traders run their WhatsApp-based businesses with speed, efficiency, and **zero hassle**.
- **Product:** a SaaS platform (web + mobile + AI + API) that turns a WhatsApp number into a full store: products, orders, payments, delivery, customers, and an AI auto-responder.
- **Markets:** Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, and beyond.
- **Scale ambition:** 1M+ users, 100+ developers, Silicon-Valley-standards engineering.

## Your onboarding buddy
You'll be assigned an **onboarding buddy** for your first 30 days. Use them for anything — questions, code reviews, logistics. If you hit friction, tell them: every complaint becomes a platform-team ticket (documented in the [friction log](../guides/development-workflow.md#8-onboarding-path)).

## First steps — today
1. Read [First week guide](./02-first-week.md) and complete [Development environment setup](./03-development-environment-setup.md).
2. Get access: GitHub, Slack, cloud read access, Sentry/Grafana logging (request via your buddy/EM).
3. Say hi in `#general` and you're off.

Welcome aboard — let's build a billion-dollar company. 🚀
